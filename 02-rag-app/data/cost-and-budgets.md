# Cost and budgets

## Monthly budget
The platform has a monthly cloud budget of 50 US dollars. An email alert is sent at 80 percent of the budget, and a second alert fires on any forecasted overspend.

## Cost tags
Every resource carries an Environment tag and a Project tag. Costs are reported per environment so that dev spending can be compared with prod.
