# Incident INC-2217

## Summary
On 14 September 2026 the dead-letter queue received 214 messages in 40 minutes. Clients saw no errors because the API accepted every request.

## Root cause
A deployment lowered the consumer timeout from 30 seconds to 3 seconds while the batch size stayed at 10. Batches that took longer than 3 seconds failed as a whole and were retried until they reached the dead-letter queue.

## Fix and follow-ups
The timeout was restored to 30 seconds and the messages were redriven after the fix. An alarm on dead-letter queue depth was added so that any message in the queue pages the on-call engineer.
